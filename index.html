How to read these results

Scope. Each dataset compares 13 other systems and two YuE2 settings. “Frontier quality” describes competitiveness with the evaluated proprietary systems under this automatic evaluation. The SOTA headline refers specifically to the highest observed SongBench mean on both datasets; it does not imply a lead on every metric, statistical significance, or established human preference.

Selection. YuE2 uses the lower phoneme error rate (PER) of two candidates. YuE2 (best-of-8) selects from eight using Musicality → Control → PER. SongBench Musicality participates in this selection, so the reported best-of-8 quality includes evaluator-based selection. ASR evaluation uses the lowest PER of four repeats per candidate. Control weights can differ between selected settings (10/192 WildSongBench records; 5/98 Old98 records).

Metrics. SongBench averages Arrangement, Instrumental, Melody, Mixing, Musicality, Structure, and Vocal. SongEval averages five quality dimensions. AudioBox PQ uses length-weighted 10-second windows, then equal song weights. MuLan and AllMusicCaps measure similarity to style text, not percentages. Control is prompt-weighted; PER is lower-is-better. All displayed aggregate metrics have complete coverage.

Scores and listening. Both evaluated YuE2 settings generate with melody-and-chord symbolic planning and use the verified new VAE for scoring. The curated Genre Explorer audio below uses the legacy VAE for listening; these previews are separate from the benchmark audio.

Plan → realize

One composition.
Many ways to make it yours.

YuE2 writes a musical score before generating the performance. A shared symbolic interface connects creation, covering, and editing.

  1. Your idea

    Lyrics + style

    Describe the song you want to hear.

  2. AR · Compose

    ABC score

    Melody, chords, tempo, meter, and form.

  3. AR · Continue

    MERT2 tokens

    A discrete semantic sequence for the performance.

  4. NAR · Realize

    Acoustic latents

    Bidirectional flow matching over the full sequence.

  5. VAE · Decode

    The full song

    Vocals and accompaniment, together.

Create: the AR stream generates the score and semantic tokens from lyrics and style. The NAR stream uses the full text, score, and semantic prefix to generate acoustic latents.

AR–NAR Mixture-of-Transformers

One backbone with separate AR and NAR normalization, projections, and MLP experts. Discrete prediction is causal; acoustic realization reads the full condition and uses bidirectional acoustic context.

An explicit musical plan

ABC notation exposes the composition for inspection and revision. MERT2 provides semantic codes from a 32,768-entry vocabulary. SheetSage2 supplies a transcribed plan when covering reference audio.

The diagram shows generation with symbolic planning. Direct generation is also supported. Editing regenerates the full song; unchanged waveform regions are not guaranteed.
