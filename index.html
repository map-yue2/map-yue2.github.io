How to read these results

            
Figure 1. Song quality combines standardized SongBench and SongEval means with weights 2:1. Text alignment equally weights standardized MuLan, AllMusicCaps, and the prompt-weighted Qwen3-Omni score. Metrics are standardized against the 13 comparator systems, then each composite is rescaled so the observed minimum and maximum are 10 and 90. These indices are not percentages. Bubble area encodes AudioBox PQ; black outlines identify Pareto optima on the two axes only.

            
Scope. WildSongBench compares 13 other systems and two YuE2 settings on 192 prompts. “Frontier quality” describes competitiveness with the evaluated proprietary systems under this automatic evaluation. The SOTA headline refers specifically to the highest observed SongBench mean in this comparison; it does not imply a lead on every metric, statistical significance, or established human preference.

            
Selection. YuE2 uses the lower phoneme error rate (PER) of two candidates. YuE2 (best-of-8) selects from eight using Musicality → Control → PER. SongBench Musicality participates in this selection, so the reported best-of-8 quality includes evaluator-based selection. ASR evaluation uses the lowest PER of four repeats per candidate. Control weights differ between selected settings in 10 of the 192 WildSongBench records.

            
Metrics. SongBench averages Arrangement, Instrumental, Melody, Mixing, Musicality, Structure, and Vocal. SongEval averages five quality dimensions. AudioBox PQ uses length-weighted 10-second windows, then equal song weights. MuLan and AllMusicCaps measure similarity to style text, not percentages. Control is prompt-weighted; PER is lower-is-better. All displayed aggregate metrics have complete coverage.

            
Scores and listening. Both evaluated YuE2 settings generate with melody-and-chord symbolic planning and use the verified new VAE for scoring. The curated Genre Explorer audio uses the legacy VAE for listening; these previews are separate from the benchmark audio.
